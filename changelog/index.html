Changelog

What's new in Subsearch, newest first. Versions follow Semantic Versioning.

0.1.0 · October 16, 2026

Added

  • Sign in with Google to download the video lists of every channel you subscribe to. Later syncs download only new videos.
  • Search video titles, descriptions, and channel names. The best matches come first: an exact title, then the full phrase in a title, then single words.
  • Sort by best match, newest, or oldest. Filter by upload date and by channel.
  • See when you last synced, and when your saved videos expire.
  • Private and deleted videos are hidden. A checkbox shows them.
  • The page address keeps your search and filters, so you can share a search as a link.
  • Choose a light or dark theme, or follow your system setting.
  • Your data stays in your browser. Data older than 30 days is deleted, and "Delete data" removes everything and signs you out.
