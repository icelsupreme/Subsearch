Subsearch v0.5.0

Privacy policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

Subsearch searches the videos of the YouTube channels you subscribe to. It runs entirely in your browser and has no server. This policy explains what data Subsearch uses and what happens to it.

Data Subsearch uses

When you sign in with Google, you give Subsearch read-only access to your YouTube account (the youtube.readonly permission). With it, Subsearch reads:

  • The list of channels you subscribe to: each channel's ID and name.
  • The public upload list of each of those channels: video IDs, titles, descriptions, upload dates, and privacy status. Subsearch saves only the first 500 characters of each description.

Subsearch doesn't read your name, email address, watch history, playlists, comments, or any other account data. It can't change anything in your account.

How Subsearch uses the data

Only to show you search results for your own subscriptions. Subsearch doesn't use the data for ads, profiling, or any other purpose.

Where the data is stored

  • The subscription and video lists are saved only in your browser's storage (IndexedDB), on your device.
  • The access token from Google stays in the page's memory during your visit and is never saved.
  • Subsearch stores your theme choice (light, dark, or system) in your browser's local storage.
  • Subsearch sets no cookies.

The developer of Subsearch never receives your data. Requests go directly from your browser to Google.

Sharing

Subsearch doesn't share, sell, or transfer your data to anyone.

How long the data is kept

  • Each channel's saved data is deleted 30 days after it was downloaded. The next sync downloads it again.
  • Click Delete data to delete all saved data from your browser and sign out.
  • Clearing your browser's site data for Subsearch also deletes everything.

Removing access

Delete data also revokes the access you gave during your visit. You can remove Subsearch's access to your Google account at any time on your Google Account permissions page.

YouTube and Google

Subsearch uses YouTube API Services. By using Subsearch, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Subsearch's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Like any website, Subsearch loads some files from other services, which receive your IP address and browser details:

  • Google Identity Services, for sign-in.
  • Google Fonts, for the page fonts.
  • YouTube's image servers, for video thumbnails.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the site.

Changes to this policy

Changes are posted on this page with a new date, and listed in the changelog.
